## 專案概述

論文專案管理工具用來協助研究者集中管理論文閱讀、研究專案與待辦事項，讓文獻整理與研究流程更容易追蹤。

## 主要功能

此工具聚焦於論文專案管理、閱讀進度追蹤、研究任務整理，以及從文獻蒐集到論文撰寫的工作流程管理。

## Overview

Paper Project Manager helps researchers organize paper reading, research projects, and task tracking in one place, making literature management and research workflows easier to follow.

## Key Features

- **Paper project management**: Organize literature by research topic or project
- **Progress tracking**: Track reading, summarizing, and writing status
- **Research workflow support**: Manage tasks from literature collection to manuscript writing
- **Practical tool focus**: Designed around repeated day-to-day research management needs
