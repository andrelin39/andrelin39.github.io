書籍簡介

本書以「跟著資料去救人」為核心理念，針對醫療從業人員設計，結合 AI 工具（ChatGPT、Claude）與 醫療資料分析（臨床大數據、生物統計），帶領讀者從零開始完成一套完整的資料分析實戰流程。

主要內容

  • AI 工具在醫療研究中的實際應用場景
  • 醫療大數據的清理、整合與分析方法
  • 臨床統計分析（生存分析、回歸模型、Meta-analysis）
  • 系統性文獻回顧與醫學寫作輔助
  • 以真實案例示範從資料到發表的完整流程

About This Book

Built around the philosophy of “following data to save lives,” this book is designed for healthcare professionals. It integrates AI tools (ChatGPT, Claude) with medical data analysis (clinical big data, biostatistics) to guide readers through a complete, practical data analysis workflow from scratch.

Key Topics

  • Real-world applications of AI tools in medical research
  • Cleaning, integration, and analysis of healthcare big data
  • Clinical statistical methods (survival analysis, regression models, meta-analysis)
  • Systematic literature review and AI-assisted medical writing
  • End-to-end case studies: from raw data to publication
