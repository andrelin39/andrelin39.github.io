<div class="lang-zh">

目前任職於**彰化基督教醫院大數據暨數位AI應用中心**，擔任研究員，專注於醫療與護理大數據分析、臨床研究設計、生物統計方法，以及人工智慧於臨床研究中的整合應用。長期協助醫療研究團隊進行資料分析、研究方法規劃與學術論文撰寫，致力於建立兼具實證基礎與臨床價值的研究模式。

此外，現任**輔英科技大學**兼任教師，教授生物統計學、醫護文獻選讀、教學原理與方法、醫療經濟學，以及大數據視覺化分析等課程。教學上重視研究思維與資料素養的培養，期望協助醫護專業人員建立從臨床問題到數據分析與學術發表的整合能力。

#### 研究興趣

我的研究興趣聚焦於醫療健康大數據、臨床研究設計、生物統計、機器學習與大型語言模型（LLM）於醫學研究中的應用，特別關注 AI 如何提升文獻回顧、資料擷取、研究品質評估與學術寫作的效率與透明性。同時，我也持續投入醫療經濟與衛生政策研究，關注醫療制度、資源配置與臨床決策之間的互動關係。

#### 目前研究方向

目前研究方向主要著重於將大型語言模型整合至臨床研究工作流程，包括系統性文獻回顧的輔助篩選、研究資料擷取自動化、統計結果語意解釋，以及 AI 輔助研究品質與報告準則檢核。期望透過人工智慧與資料科學的結合，建立更高效率、可重現且具臨床實務價值的研究流程，並推動醫療研究從傳統經驗導向逐步邁向資料驅動與智慧化決策。

<div class="lang-en">

I am a Researcher at the **Big Data & Digital AI Application Center, Changhua Christian Hospital**, specializing in healthcare and nursing big data analytics, clinical research design, biostatistical methodology, and the integration of artificial intelligence into clinical research. I provide long-term support to medical research teams in data analysis, research methodology planning, and academic manuscript preparation, with a commitment to building evidence-based, clinically meaningful research models.

I also serve as an Adjunct Lecturer at **Fooyin University**, teaching Biostatistics, Medical Literature Review, Teaching Principles and Methods, Health Economics, and Big Data Visualization. My teaching emphasizes the cultivation of research thinking and data literacy, with the goal of helping healthcare professionals develop integrated competencies — from clinical question formulation through data analysis to academic publication.

#### Research Interests

My research interests center on healthcare big data, clinical research design, biostatistics, machine learning, and the application of large language models (LLMs) in medical research — with particular attention to how AI can improve the efficiency and transparency of literature review, data extraction, research quality assessment, and academic writing. I also engage in health economics and healthcare policy research, examining the interplay among healthcare systems, resource allocation, and clinical decision-making.

#### Current Focus

My current work focuses on integrating large language models into clinical research workflows, including AI-assisted screening for systematic reviews, automated research data extraction, semantic interpretation of statistical results, and AI-supported research quality and reporting guideline verification. Through the combination of artificial intelligence and data science, I aim to establish research pipelines that are more efficient, reproducible, and clinically relevant — advancing medical research from traditional experience-driven approaches toward data-driven and intelligent decision-making.
