<div class="lang-zh" markdown="1">

任職於**彰化基督教醫院**大數據暨數位AI應用中心（研究員），同時擔任**輔英科技大學**兼任教師。專注於醫療大數據分析、臨床研究設計與生物統計，以及 AI 於臨床研究的整合應用；長期協助醫療研究團隊進行資料分析、方法規劃與學術論文撰寫。

**研究興趣**　醫療大數據・臨床研究設計・生物統計・機器學習・LLM 醫學應用・醫療經濟與衛生政策

**目前方向**　將大型語言模型整合至臨床研究工作流程——系統性文獻回顧輔助篩選、資料擷取自動化、統計結果語意解釋，以及 AI 輔助研究品質評估，致力推動醫療研究邁向資料驅動與智慧化決策。

<div class="lang-en" markdown="1">

Researcher at the **Big Data & Digital AI Application Center, Changhua Christian Hospital**, and Adjunct Lecturer at **Fooyin University**. My work spans healthcare big data analytics, clinical research design, biostatistics, and AI integration in clinical research. I provide ongoing support to medical research teams in data analysis, methodology planning, and academic writing.

**Research Interests**　Healthcare Big Data · Clinical Research Design · Biostatistics · Machine Learning · LLM in Medicine · Health Economics & Policy

**Current Focus**　Integrating large language models into clinical research workflows — AI-assisted systematic review screening, automated data extraction, semantic interpretation of statistical results, and AI-supported research quality assessment — toward efficient, reproducible, data-driven medical research.
